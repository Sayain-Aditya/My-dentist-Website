What are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are the gold standard for permanent tooth replacement. They consist of a titanium post surgically placed into your jawbone, which acts as an artificial tooth root. Once the implant fuses with the bone (osseointegration), a custom crown is attached, giving you a tooth that looks, feels, and functions just like your natural teeth.

Why Choose Dental Implants?

Dental implants are recommended when you have:

  • Missing teeth due to decay, injury, or extraction
  • Loose dentures that cause discomfort or embarrassment
  • Bone loss in the jaw from missing teeth
  • Difficulty chewing or speaking due to tooth loss
  • Desire for permanent solution instead of removable dentures
  • Good oral health and sufficient jawbone density

Dental Implant Procedure

Our dental implant procedure in Lucknow follows a systematic, proven approach:

Step 1: Consultation & Planning
Comprehensive examination including CT scan and 3D imaging to assess bone quality and plan implant placement. We discuss your goals and create a customized treatment plan.

Step 2: Bone Grafting (If Needed)
If you have insufficient bone density, we may perform bone grafting to strengthen the jawbone. This ensures successful implant integration.

Step 3: Implant Placement Surgery
Under local anesthesia, the titanium implant post is surgically placed into your jawbone. The procedure is virtually painless and takes 1-2 hours per implant.

Step 4: Healing Period (Osseointegration)
Over 3-6 months, the implant fuses with your jawbone through a natural process called osseointegration. A temporary crown may be placed during this time.

Step 5: Abutment Placement
Once healing is complete, an abutment (connector piece) is attached to the implant. This serves as the foundation for your crown.

Step 6: Crown Fabrication & Placement
Custom porcelain or zirconia crown is created to match your natural teeth perfectly. The crown is then permanently attached to the abutment.

Step 7: Follow-Up Care
Regular check-ups ensure your implant remains healthy and functional. With proper care, your implant can last a lifetime.

Types of Dental Implants We Offer

1. Single Tooth Implant
Replaces one missing tooth with an implant, abutment, and crown. Ideal for individual tooth loss.

2. Multiple Tooth Implants
Uses 2-3 implants to support a bridge for multiple missing teeth. More stable than traditional bridges.

3. All-on-4 Implants
Full arch restoration using just 4 strategically placed implants. Cost-effective solution for complete tooth loss.

4. All-on-6 Implants
Enhanced stability with 6 implants supporting a full arch of teeth. Ideal for patients with moderate bone loss.

5. Implant-Supported Dentures
Removable dentures secured by 2-4 implants. More stable than traditional dentures at a lower cost than fixed implants.

Dental Implants vs Other Options

Implants vs Dentures: Implants are permanent, stable, and don't slip. Dentures are removable and less comfortable.

Implants vs Bridges: Implants don't require grinding down healthy teeth. Bridges need adjacent teeth for support.

Implants vs Doing Nothing: Missing teeth cause bone loss, shifting teeth, and bite problems. Implants prevent these issues.
