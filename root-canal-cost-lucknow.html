Factors That Affect Root Canal Cost in Lucknow

  • Tooth location — Molars have more canals and cost more than front teeth
  • Severity of infection — Advanced infections may require additional treatment
  • Number of sittings — Single-sitting RCT is available for suitable cases
  • Technology used — Rotary endodontics costs slightly more but is faster and more precise
  • Crown placement — A crown after RCT adds ₹2,000–₹8,000 to the total cost
  • Dentist experience — Specialist endodontists may charge more

Root Canal vs Tooth Extraction — Which is Better?

Saving your natural tooth is always the preferred option. Root canal treatment preserves your original tooth structure, maintains your bite, and prevents bone loss. Extraction followed by a dental implant in Lucknow costs significantly more in the long run. Always choose RCT when possible.

Post Root Canal Care

  • Avoid chewing on the treated tooth until the crown is placed
  • Take prescribed medications as directed
  • Maintain good oral hygiene — brush twice daily and floss
  • Attend follow-up appointments as scheduled
  • Get a crown placed within 2–4 weeks of RCT completion
