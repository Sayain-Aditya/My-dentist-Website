What is Flap Surgery?

Flap surgery, also known as periodontal flap surgery or pocket reduction surgery, is an advanced dental procedure designed to treat severe gum disease (periodontitis). When gum disease progresses beyond the early stages, deep pockets form between teeth and gums, harboring bacteria that cannot be removed through regular cleaning or scaling.

Why Do You Need Flap Surgery?

Flap surgery becomes necessary when you have:

  • Deep periodontal pockets (5mm or deeper) that cannot be cleaned with regular methods
  • Advanced gum disease with bone loss around teeth
  • Persistent gum infections despite scaling and root planing
  • Gum recession exposing tooth roots
  • Loose teeth due to bone and tissue loss
  • Chronic bad breath from deep bacterial pockets
  • Bleeding gums that don't improve with treatment

Flap Surgery Procedure

Our periodontal flap surgery in Lucknow follows a precise, systematic approach:

Step 1: Pre-Surgical Evaluation
Comprehensive examination with X-rays and periodontal probing to assess pocket depth, bone loss, and treatment needs. We create a customized surgical plan.

Step 2: Local Anesthesia
Advanced local anesthesia is administered to ensure complete comfort during the procedure. You'll feel no pain throughout the surgery.

Step 3: Flap Creation
Small incisions are made in the gum tissue, and the gums are gently lifted back (like a flap) to expose the tooth roots and underlying bone.

Step 4: Deep Cleaning
Tartar, plaque, and infected tissue are thoroughly removed from tooth roots and bone surfaces. Root surfaces are smoothed (root planing) to prevent bacterial reattachment.

Step 5: Bone Reshaping/Grafting
If necessary, irregular bone surfaces are reshaped, or bone grafts are placed to regenerate lost bone and provide better support for teeth.

Step 6: Flap Repositioning
The gum flaps are repositioned snugly around the teeth at a reduced pocket depth and secured with sutures for optimal healing.

Step 7: Post-Operative Care
Protective dressing may be applied. Detailed aftercare instructions and medications are provided for comfortable healing.

Post-Surgery Care

After your flap surgery, follow these guidelines for optimal healing:

  • Take prescribed antibiotics and pain medications as directed
  • Apply ice packs to reduce swelling in the first 24 hours
  • Eat soft, cool foods for the first few days
  • Avoid hot, spicy, or hard foods that may irritate surgical sites
  • Don't brush surgical areas for the first week; use prescribed mouthwash
  • Avoid smoking and alcohol during healing period
  • Attend all follow-up appointments for suture removal and monitoring
  • Maintain excellent oral hygiene after healing to prevent recurrence

Recovery Timeline

First 24-48 hours: Mild discomfort, swelling, and bleeding are normal. Use ice packs and take medications as prescribed.

Week 1: Swelling subsides. Soft diet continues. Avoid brushing surgical areas. Use prescribed mouthwash.

Week 2: Sutures removed. Gums begin healing. Can resume gentle brushing with soft toothbrush.

Weeks 3-4: Significant healing occurs. Gums firm up. Can gradually return to normal diet and oral hygiene.

Months 1-3: Complete healing and tissue maturation. Regular dental cleanings resume. Long-term maintenance begins.

Preventing Gum Disease Recurrence

After successful flap surgery, maintain your results with:

  • Excellent oral hygiene: Brush twice daily and floss once daily without fail
  • Regular dental visits: Professional cleanings every 3-4 months
  • Quit smoking: Smoking is the #1 risk factor for gum disease recurrence
  • Healthy diet: Eat nutritious foods rich in vitamins C and D for gum health
  • Manage health conditions: Control diabetes and other systemic conditions
  • Use antimicrobial mouthwash: As recommended by your dentist
