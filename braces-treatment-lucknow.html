What is Braces Treatment?

Braces treatment, also known as orthodontic treatment, is a dental procedure that uses brackets, wires, and bands to gradually straighten misaligned teeth and correct bite problems. Braces apply gentle, continuous pressure to move teeth into their ideal positions over time, giving you a beautiful, healthy smile.

At My Dentist Multispeciality Dental Clinic in Lalbagh, Lucknow, we specialize in comprehensive orthodontic treatment using both traditional metal braces and aesthetic ceramic braces. Our clinic serves patients from Hazratganj, Gomti Nagar, and across Lucknow with expert orthodontic care.

Why Do You Need Braces Treatment?

Braces treatment is recommended for:

  • Crooked or crowded teeth that overlap or twist
  • Gaps between teeth (diastema)
  • Overbite (upper teeth protrude over lower teeth)
  • Underbite (lower teeth protrude beyond upper teeth)
  • Crossbite (upper and lower teeth don't align properly)
  • Open bite (front teeth don't touch when mouth is closed)
  • Jaw misalignment causing difficulty chewing or speaking
  • Aesthetic concerns about smile appearance

Types of Braces We Offer

1. Metal Braces (Traditional Braces)
The most common and cost-effective option. Made of high-grade stainless steel brackets and wires. Highly effective for all types of misalignment. Treatment duration: 12-24 months.

2. Ceramic Braces (Tooth-Colored Braces)
Made of clear or tooth-colored ceramic material that blends with your natural teeth. Less visible than metal braces. Ideal for adults and teens concerned about aesthetics. Slightly more expensive than metal braces.

3. Self-Ligating Braces
Use a specialized clip instead of elastic bands to hold the wire. Require fewer adjustments and appointments. More comfortable with less friction. Faster treatment in some cases.

Braces Treatment Procedure

Our braces treatment in Lucknow follows a systematic approach:

Step 1: Initial Consultation
Comprehensive examination including X-rays, photographs, and dental impressions. We assess your teeth alignment, bite, and jaw relationship to create a customized treatment plan.

Step 2: Treatment Planning
We discuss treatment options (metal vs ceramic braces), expected duration, and cost. Digital treatment simulation shows you the expected results.

Step 3: Teeth Preparation
Professional cleaning to ensure brackets bond properly. Tooth extraction may be recommended if severe crowding exists (not always necessary).

Step 4: Braces Placement
Brackets are bonded to each tooth using dental adhesive. Archwire is threaded through brackets and secured with elastic bands. The entire process takes 1-2 hours and is painless.

Step 5: Regular Adjustments
Monthly appointments (every 4-6 weeks) for wire adjustments and progress monitoring. Each adjustment takes 15-30 minutes. Elastic bands are changed at each visit.

Step 6: Braces Removal
Once treatment goals are achieved (12-24 months), braces are carefully removed. Teeth are polished to remove any adhesive residue.

Step 7: Retention Phase
Retainers are provided to maintain teeth in their new positions. Wear retainers as directed (usually full-time for 6 months, then nights only). Retention is crucial to prevent teeth from shifting back.

How Long Does Braces Treatment Take?

Treatment duration varies based on individual cases:

  • Minor misalignment: 12-15 months
  • Moderate cases: 15-18 months
  • Severe misalignment: 18-24 months
  • Complex cases: 24-30 months

Factors affecting treatment duration include age, severity of misalignment, type of braces, and patient compliance with wearing elastics and attending appointments.

Life with Braces: What to Expect

First Few Days: Mild soreness and discomfort for 2-3 days after placement and adjustments. Over-the-counter pain relievers help.

Eating: Avoid hard, sticky, and chewy foods (nuts, popcorn, caramel, gum). Cut food into small pieces. Soft foods recommended initially.

Oral Hygiene: Brush after every meal using a soft-bristled brush. Floss daily with floss threaders or water flossers. Use fluoride mouthwash.

Adjustments: Monthly appointments for wire changes and progress checks. Each visit takes 15-30 minutes.

Emergencies: Broken brackets or poking wires can be temporarily fixed with orthodontic wax. Contact us for repair.

Foods to Avoid with Braces

  • Hard foods: nuts, hard candy, ice, popcorn
  • Sticky foods: caramel, toffee, chewing gum
  • Chewy foods: bagels, licorice, tough meat
  • Crunchy foods: chips, hard cookies, raw carrots
  • Foods you bite into: apples, corn on the cob (cut first)

Braces for Adults vs Children

Children (Ages 10-14): Ideal age for braces as jaw is still growing. Faster treatment with better results. Prevents future dental problems.

Teens (Ages 15-19): Most common age group for braces. Aesthetic options like ceramic braces available. Treatment takes 12-24 months.

Adults (20+): Never too late for braces! Treatment may take slightly longer. Ceramic braces or clear aligners preferred for aesthetics. Improves oral health and confidence.

Braces vs Invisalign

Braces: More effective for complex cases. Cannot be removed. More affordable. Visible (unless ceramic).

Invisalign: Nearly invisible. Removable for eating and cleaning. More expensive. Best for mild to moderate cases.

We offer both options. Schedule a consultation to determine which is best for you.
