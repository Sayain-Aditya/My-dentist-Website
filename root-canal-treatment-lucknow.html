What is Root Canal Treatment?

Root canal treatment (RCT), also known as endodontic therapy, is a dental procedure designed to save a severely infected or damaged tooth. When the pulp (the soft tissue inside your tooth containing nerves and blood vessels) becomes infected due to deep decay, cracks, or trauma, a root canal becomes necessary to prevent tooth loss.

Why Do You Need Root Canal Treatment?

Root canal treatment becomes necessary when:

  • Deep tooth decay reaches the pulp chamber
  • Severe toothache that doesn't go away with painkillers
  • Tooth abscess or pus formation around the tooth
  • Sensitivity to hot and cold that lingers
  • Tooth trauma from accidents or injury
  • Swelling in gums near the affected tooth
  • Discoloration or darkening of the tooth

Root Canal Treatment Procedure

Our painless root canal treatment in Lucknow follows a systematic, comfortable approach:

Step 1: Diagnosis & X-Ray
We take digital X-rays to assess the extent of infection and plan the treatment. Our dentist examines the tooth and surrounding structures to determine the best approach.

Step 2: Local Anesthesia
Advanced local anesthesia is administered to ensure you feel no pain during the procedure. We use the latest anesthetic techniques for maximum comfort.

Step 3: Isolation & Access
A rubber dam is placed to isolate the tooth and keep it dry. A small opening is made in the crown to access the infected pulp chamber.

Step 4: Cleaning & Shaping
Using advanced rotary endodontic instruments, we carefully remove the infected pulp tissue, clean the root canals, and shape them for filling. This is the most critical step.

Step 5: Disinfection
The canals are thoroughly disinfected with antimicrobial solutions to eliminate all bacteria and prevent reinfection.

Step 6: Filling & Sealing
The cleaned canals are filled with biocompatible gutta-percha material and sealed to prevent future infection. A temporary filling is placed.

Step 7: Crown Placement
After healing (usually 1-2 weeks), a permanent crown is placed to restore the tooth's strength, function, and appearance.

Post-Treatment Care

After your root canal treatment, follow these guidelines for optimal healing:

  • Avoid chewing on the treated tooth until the permanent crown is placed
  • Take prescribed medications as directed
  • Maintain excellent oral hygiene with gentle brushing and flossing
  • Attend follow-up appointments as scheduled
  • Report any unusual pain, swelling, or discomfort immediately
  • Avoid hard, sticky foods for the first few days
